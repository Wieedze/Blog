So far I've shipped a product to production with real users (Sofia), landed a
grant, delivered a paid mission for a DAO, and started several projects. The
tempting move would be to inflate that into "expertise." I'd rather do the
opposite.

## The posture: exploration, not expertise

A developer profile that claims to have mastered everything rings false, and
people feel it. What's true, and more interesting, is the **learning speed** and
the ability to turn an idea into something that runs.

So here I document:

- what I'm building and why,
- what works,
- what gets stuck (and there's plenty of that),
- what I learn along the way.

## What this site is not

It's not a frozen résumé. It's a logbook. The [projects](/projects) page shows
the real state of things; the [now](/now) page says what I'm on today; the
[activity](/activity) feed proves it's moving, without me having to claim it.

## What's next

More products, in the open. New bets, and honest write-ups when they work and
when they don't. All of it gets told here, as it happens.
