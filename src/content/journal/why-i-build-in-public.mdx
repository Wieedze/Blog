I've been coding for a year. Put that way, it's short. But in a year I shipped a
product to production with real users (Sofia), landed a grant, won a contract
with a DAO, and started several projects — including an MVP this week.

The tempting move would be to inflate all of that into "expertise." I'd rather do
the opposite.

## The posture: exploration, not expertise

A developer profile that claims to have mastered everything one year into a
career rings false, and people feel it. What's true — and more interesting — is
the **learning speed** and the ability to turn an idea into something that runs.

So here I document:

- what I'm building and why,
- what works,
- what gets stuck (and there's plenty of that),
- what I learn along the way.

## What this site is not

It's not a frozen résumé. It's a logbook. The [projects](/projects) page shows
the real state of things; the [now](/now) page says what I'm on today; the
[activity](/activity) feed proves it's moving, without me having to claim it.

## What's next

Heading to ETHGlobal Lisbon with ARP, learning ZK with Noir, and looking for a
team to go further. All of it will be told here, as it happens.
