Today we're starting [Sofia](/projects/sofia). Two of us, as the graduation
project for our web-dev degree, but we're not treating it like a school
assignment. The bet is bigger than the grade.

The premise is simple to say and hard to build: take the signals a person
already generates (what they do, where, how consistently) and turn them into
**reputation that lives on-chain**, not in a private database we happen to
control. On Intuition that means the atom/triple model: identities and claims
become objects you can point at, attest to, and build on top of.

## The bet

We think reputation should be *structured claims*, not a score. A number is
opaque; a triple ("this subject, did, this thing") is legible: you can argue
with it, extend it, or stake on it. Starting from claims instead of a single
figure feels like the more honest foundation. We'll find out if it holds.

## What we expect to be hard

Onboarding. Mapping messy human activity onto clean attestations is where the
difficulty will hide: every edge case is a small judgment call about what a
signal *means*. And restraint: an attestation is public and durable, so the cost
of getting the model wrong is higher than in a normal app. That changes how you
build.

## Why in public

Sofia is the start of this journal, and the spine the rest will hang off: the
same atom/triple thinking will show up later in work on AI agents and on
Circles. We don't know yet what it becomes. That's exactly why it's worth
writing down from day one.
